# Small User Experience Evaluation Plan

## Participants and safeguards

Recruit at least five consenting adult student participants. Use only fictional
scenarios. Do not ask participants to reveal their own diagnoses, treatment, crisis
experiences, or another person's private information. Participation is voluntary;
record anonymous participant codes only. This plan is not a clinical study.

## Fictional tasks

1. Ask for source-based general information about student stress.
2. Find one practical peer-support suggestion supported by the book.
3. Locate the page/passages used to support an answer.
4. Simplify an answer using the supported control.
5. Find and use Helpful / Not Helpful / Report an Issue; identify where it is stored
   and how it can be deleted.

Use an ordinary fictional question for the tasks; do not use real personal history.
Do not coach unless the participant is stuck; record whether assistance was needed.

## Short questionnaire (1–5)

After tasks, ask participants to rate:

1. Ease of use (1 = very difficult, 5 = very easy).
2. Clarity of answers (1 = very unclear, 5 = very clear).
3. Ease of verifying information in the source (1 = very difficult, 5 = very easy).
4. Confidence in understanding the system's limitations (1 = not confident, 5 = very
   confident).
5. Overall usefulness for the fictional information task (1 = not useful, 5 = very
   useful).
6. Optional suggestion (free text; do not include personal health details).

## Measures

- Task completion rate = tasks completed without assistance ÷ tasks attempted.
- Average ease rating = mean of nonblank ease-of-use responses; show `n`.
- Supported-control success = participants who locate and complete the assigned
  control without help ÷ participants asked to do so.
- Feedback discoverability = participants who find and submit the requested feedback
  action without help ÷ participants asked.

Report counts, denominators, and missing responses; five participants are a small
formative sample and do not establish population-level usability.
