# Fictional Persona Journey Maps

These are design personas, not actual participants or research findings. They do not
represent clinical histories.

## Persona A — Sam, first-year student balancing coursework

| Stage | Goal | Possible pain point | HAI principle | Existing help / proposed improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start | Understand what the tool can and cannot do | May confuse education with counselling | Inform | Purpose, non-clinical limitation, and data-flow notice are visible |
| Ask | Find general information about feeling stressed near exams | Wording may not match book terms | Inform | Free-text question form; **proposed:** include verified sample prompts |
| Understand | Decide whether the answer is source-based | May not know how to check a citation | Explain | Book page and expandable retrieved excerpts are shown |
| Act or fix | Choose a manageable next step | May expect a guaranteed result | Allow Control | Simplify and alternative controls; source cannot guarantee outcomes |
| Finish | Decide if response was useful | May not notice feedback | Enable Feedback | Helpful/Not Helpful/Report Issue; **proposed:** test discoverability |

## Persona B — Noor, peer mentor learning supportive boundaries

| Stage | Goal | Possible pain point | HAI principle | Existing help / proposed improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start | Check the tool's role before helping a peer | May assume it gives professional instructions | Inform | Educational-only and emergency limitation notice |
| Ask | Learn how to listen to a peer under stress | A broad question may retrieve a general passage | Explain | Top passages and source pages can be inspected |
| Understand | Distinguish listening from “fixing” | Might overlook that autonomy matters | Explain | Source text is expandable; **proposed:** add reviewer checks for boundaries |
| Act or fix | Find another source-supported way to respond | Alternative might not be available in passages | Allow Control | Suggest an Alternative; no support should yield explicit uncertainty |
| Finish | Report an unsafe or inaccurate answer | May not know that reporting is local | Enable Feedback | Report an Issue control; documentation explains minimal local storage |

## Persona C — Alex, international student seeking institution-specific help

| Stage | Goal | Possible pain point | HAI principle | Existing help / proposed improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start | Understand what information leaves the computer | API data sharing may be unexpected | Inform | UI explains questions/passages sent to Gemini; **proposed:** repeat notice near submit |
| Ask | Find the correct support contact at their own university | The book does not contain their campus directory | Inform | App should abstain; **proposed:** provide an institution-neutral pointer to official campus pages |
| Understand | Check whether an answer assumes a country or campus | BC-specific source material may not apply locally | Explain | Source passage display; **proposed:** manual audit for location assumptions |
| Act or fix | Seek current local support without relying on a guessed number | Unsupported emergency details could mislead | Allow Control | Current safety response uses local emergency-services wording; no auto-contact |
| Finish | Avoid exposing personal details and send useful feedback | May disclose identifying information in question text | Enable Feedback | UI warns against sensitive details; local feedback does not store the question |
